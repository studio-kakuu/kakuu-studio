KAKUU OS is a dashboard of the system that actually makes KAKUU STUDIO's work: Planning, Design, Build, Motion, Publish and Reach. One line of request travels through six units, and a human approves before anything is posted.

Every number on screen is counted from the real repository (as of Day 1): 7 changes merged today, 21 official skills, 3 videos exported.
